## Natural Science Core Subjects

Physics 
 - The study of matter, energy, and the laws of nature.

Chemistry
 - The study of substances, their properties, and reactions.

Biology
 - The study of living organisms and life processes.

Astronomy
 - The study of celestial bodies and the universe.

Earth Science
 - The study of the Earth: geology, meteorology, oceanography, etc.
